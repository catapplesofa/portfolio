## Description
 The idea of turning the Six Realms into a game has long been something Ben, our co-founder, wanted to explore. During a jam, I imagined using a dream background to narrate and connect the realms, and decided to continue developing the idea afterward. From June to August, the Karma team finished designing Part I of the Hungry Ghost realm, which includes three quests, and we are now working on the MVP.
## My Role
 I design the core narrative storyline and game mechanics with Abhilasha (another intern), and contribute concept art when needed.
## Highlights
- Research on hungry ghosts:
 Because our message focuses on getting out of the hungry ghost realm—a place filled with ghosts who crave food but can never be satisfied—I researched different interpretations of the realm through parables, scholars, and monks. From this, I identified three implications we can learn from at the individual, family, and community levels to prevent falling into the hungry ghost state.

- Transforming ideas into games:
 After capturing the abstract messages we wanted to convey, I worked on turning them into concrete stories, characters, and locations through discussions with Abhi. We then added game mechanisms to make it interactive—things like breathing practices with a monk, a reward system, karma calculations, and “wisdom cards” with Buddhist sayings.

- Scene and character treatments:
 With my art background, I created concept art for the main scene and character designs by making digital drafts and then using ChatGPT to help me polish them.

- Opening scene:
 Lastly, Abhi and I designed the opening scene to welcome players, writing a relatable story about the main character and how they fall into a dream and enter the Six Realms.
## Outcome
 We designed Part I of the Hungry Ghost realm, created a prototype of the game, and collected user feedback across different teams in the Verse.
