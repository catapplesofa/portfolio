## Description
In the final project of JPN118 Murakami Haruki and World Literature, I created a painting in Chinese ink to respond to the idea that reality is a reflection of the mind. This idea is expressed through the contemplation of the protagonists, Aomame and Tengo, on their past experiences, current behaviors, and beliefs in the parallel universe of 1Q84 and 1984. The idea that the mind and the reality spontaneously appear in reflection to each other leads to self-determinism, expressed by Aomame. Each individual is determined by their own course of life. 

## My Role
This is an independent project.

## Highlights

- The Creation of Tree
After chatting with Mr. Cui, my Chinese ink painting teacher, I was surprised to learn that traditional Chinese artists have been expressing the relationship between the mind and reality through shadows and composition in mountain paintings. Eager to explore such expression, I chose a scene on campus of a tree with abundant and balanced layers of yellow flowers and green leaves. 

![tree]({{ "/assets/images/1q84tree.jpg" | relative_url }})


The painting responds to the idea of mind and reality in 1Q84 in the following ways:

- Green and Yellow - Two moons  
The coexistence of green leaves and yellow flowers echoes the color of the two moons in 1Q84. Furthermore, the state of coexistence also challenges the question of “which comes first?” The cycle of the transition from leaves to flowers, and vice versa challenges the linearity of time.

![detail1]({{ "/assets/images/greenyellow.png" | relative_url }})

- Lighting and Shadow - Reality and the Mind  
The symmetry of lighting and shadow symbolizes how external reality and internal perception coexist and reflect each other.   

![detail2]({{ "/assets/images/greenyellow.jpg" | relative_url }})
![detail2]({{ "/assets/images/symmetric.jpg" | relative_url }})

- Repetition - Maza and Dota  
Each area of light and shadow further divide into smaller areas of light and shadow. Such repetition echoes “maza” and “dota” in 1Q84, where the “dota” is a duplicate of the “maza”.

![detail3]({{ "/assets/images/repetition.jpg" | relative_url }})
