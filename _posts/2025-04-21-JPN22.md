## Description
During the individual project component of JPN22, I made a video with Procreate inspired by 「一つの星とともに」(With One Star) by Nakabayashi Eiko. In her Poetry Selection, Nakabayashi wrote about the realization of the constant changes through observations of objects, plants and people. She expressed a desire to connect with infinity, represented by the star, through poetry. Her poems are made up of both detailed scenes, metaphors, and abstract feelings. In the video, I incorporated scenes described by Nakabayashi, like the tree that outlived her father, abstract feelings such as “transpassing time”, and my own imagination of Nakabayashi’s pursuit. This is also the first time that I make an animation.
