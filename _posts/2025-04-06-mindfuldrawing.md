## Description
As a member of the eboard of Tufts Mindfulness Buddhism Sangha, I planned a mindful drawing experience to introduce spontaneous drawing to a larger audience. An area next to a beautiful view was set up for drawing with paper on the wall and drawing materials.  
![overview]({{ "/assets/images/mdoverview.jpg" | relative_url }})

Retreaters are encouraged to draw whatever they want to at the present moment in any time during the retreat.
![dao]({{ "/assets/images/mddao.jpg" | relative_url }})
![detail1]({{ "/assets/images/mddetail1.jpg" | relative_url }})

With “concentrating on the present moment ” as the only prompt, the mindful drawing corner provided a space for retreaters to loosen, express, retrieve their passion in drawing, and communicate with each other.
![4paper]({{ "/assets/images/md4.jpg" | relative_url }})
